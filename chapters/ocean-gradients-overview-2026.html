An ocean of gradients – towards a 3D mapping and visualization of high-resolution marine data: An overview

What this chapter covers

The chapter frames the scientific and methodological challenges of moving from sparse or discipline-specific measurements toward interoperable, high-resolution three-dimensional descriptions of marine systems.

When this chapter may be useful

  • Marine environmental gradients
  • High-resolution marine observations
  • Interdisciplinary marine data integration
  • 3D mapping and visualisation
