State-space models and imputation of environmental time series

Environmental and palaeoclimate records are often irregularly sampled and can contain long gaps. State-space models separate a latent environmental trajectory from observation noise, providing a natural framework for smoothing, interpolation and uncertainty-aware reconstruction.

Lake Ohrid isotope gaps

A latent Gaussian state-space model regularises multiple irregular proxy series before SSVS regression is used to reconstruct missing δ18O and δ13C values.
