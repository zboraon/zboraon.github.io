Bayesian change-point analysis for environmental records

Change-point methods estimate where a time series changes rather than fixing the transition date in advance. Bayesian formulations express transition timing as a posterior distribution and can share information across multiple sites or proxies.

A fully Bayesian model tests the presence and timing of changes across regional palaeoclimate records.
