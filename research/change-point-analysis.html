Bayesian change-point analysis for environmental records

Change-point methods estimate where a time series changes rather than requiring the analyst to fix the transition date in advance. In palaeoclimate and stratigraphic applications, a Bayesian formulation is particularly useful because the timing of a transition can be expressed as a posterior distribution rather than a single deterministic date.

4.2 ka BP event

A fully Bayesian change-point analysis tests the presence and timing of abrupt changes across records from southeast Europe and southwest Asia.
