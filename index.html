Zeki Bora Ön

I develop and apply Bayesian and stochastic methods to palaeoclimate and environmental records, with particular interests in state-space modelling, change-point detection, hierarchical models, uncertainty quantification and incomplete proxy time series.

Publications ORCID Google Scholar GitHub

Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Muğla Sıtkı Koçman University, Türkiye. My academic background combines mathematics, Earth sciences and statistical modelling.

Research themes

Bayesian palaeoclimate reconstruction

Probabilistic reconstruction of climate histories from proxy records while carrying calibration, observational and model uncertainty through the analysis.

Bayesian inferencepalaeoclimateuncertainty

Change-point analysis

Identifying and dating abrupt or gradual environmental transitions without fixing change points in advance, including hierarchical treatment of multiple records.

MCMCchange pointsabrupt events

State-space & missing-data models

Regularising irregular environmental records and reconstructing missing proxy observations through latent-state models, multivariate evidence and explicit uncertainty propagation.
