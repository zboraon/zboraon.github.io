Bayesian statistics · palaeoclimate · environmental time series

Zeki Bora Ön

I develop and apply Bayesian and stochastic methods to palaeoclimate and environmental records, with particular interests in state-space modelling, change-point detection, hierarchical models, uncertainty quantification, missing data and quantitative proxy extraction.

Publications ORCIDGoogle ScholarGitHub

Associate Professor, Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering and Geological Engineering, Muğla Sıtkı Koçman University, Türkiye. My academic background combines mathematics, Earth sciences and statistical modelling.
