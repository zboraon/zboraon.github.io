Research article · 2021

High-resolution temperature and precipitation variability of southwest Anatolia since 1730 CE from Lake Gölcük sedimentary records

Iliya Bauchi Danladi, Sena Akçer-Ön, Zeki Bora Ön, Sabine Schmidt · Turkish Journal of Earth Sciences 30(5), 601–610 (2021)

Publisher / DOIFull text (PDF)

What this study does

The study analyses two short sediment cores with high-resolution geochemical and chronological information to reconstruct recent hydroclimate and temperature variability.

Main finding

Wetter intervals broadly correspond to negative NAO phases and/or reduced solar activity, whereas drier intervals often align with positive NAO conditions and higher solar activity, with some notable exceptions.

When this paper may be useful

  • Recent-centuries palaeoclimate in Anatolia
  • Lake-sediment calibration against atmospheric circulation
  • NAO–hydroclimate comparisons
  • High-resolution environmental time series
