Research article · 2023

A Late Holocene climate reconstruction from the high-altitude Lake Gölcük sedimentary records, Isparta (SW Anatolia)

Iliya Bauchi Danladi, Sena Akçer-Ön, Thomas Litt, Zeki Bora Ön, Lukas Wacker · Quaternary Research 115, 120–133 (2023)

Publisher / DOI

What this study does

The study uses sedimentological, geochemical, magnetic and stable-isotope evidence, supported by radiocarbon chronology, to reconstruct hydroclimate and temperature variability from a high-altitude lake archive.

Main finding

The Lake Gölcük record resolves centennial-scale late-Holocene variability and allows comparison with large-scale atmospheric circulation and solar forcing, providing regional context for climate changes since roughly the first millennium CE.

When this paper may be useful

  • Late-Holocene climate in southwestern Anatolia
  • High-resolution lake-sediment multiproxy studies
  • Regional comparison with NAO and solar variability
  • Stable-isotope and μXRF palaeoclimate work
