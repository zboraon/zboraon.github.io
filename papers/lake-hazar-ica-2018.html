Research article · 2018

Climate proxies for the last 17.3 ka from Lake Hazar (Eastern Anatolia), extracted by independent component analysis of μ-XRF data

Zeki Bora Ön, Sena Akçer-Ön, M. Sinan Özeren, K. Kadir Eriş, Alan M. Greaves, M. Namık Çağatay · Quaternary International 486, 17–28 (2018)

Publisher / DOIDataFull text (Google Drive)

What this study does

The paper applies independent component analysis to multielement μ-XRF records as a data-driven method for separating mixed geochemical signals into interpretable climate proxies.

Main finding

The extracted components resolve major late-glacial and Holocene hydroclimate changes, including transitions around the Bølling–Allerød, Younger Dryas and early-to-mid Holocene.

When this paper may be useful

  • Extracting climate proxies from multivariate μXRF records
  • Independent component analysis in sediment geochemistry
  • Late-glacial and Holocene hydroclimate
  • Data-driven proxy separation
