Research article · 2023

A novel Bayesian multilevel regression approach to the reconstruction of an eastern Mediterranean temperature record for the last 10,000 years

Zeki Bora Ön, Neil Macdonald, Sena Akçer-Ön, Alan M. Greaves · The Holocene 33(7), 807–815 (2023)

Publisher / DOICodeFull text (PDF)

What this study does

The paper combines 33 previously published proxy-based reconstructions using Bayesian multilevel regression in consecutive time intervals, allowing records to contribute to a regional estimate while retaining between-record variability.

Main finding

The framework produces a regional Holocene temperature history with explicit posterior uncertainty and demonstrates a reproducible statistical route for synthesising heterogeneous palaeoclimate reconstructions.

When this paper may be useful

  • Regional synthesis of heterogeneous palaeoclimate records
  • Hierarchical or multilevel Bayesian regression
  • Combining site-level reconstructions with uncertainty
  • Holocene temperature reconstruction
