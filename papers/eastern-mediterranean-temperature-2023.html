Research article · 2023

A novel Bayesian multilevel regression approach to the reconstruction of an eastern Mediterranean temperature record for the last 10,000 years

Zeki Bora Ön, Neil Macdonald, Sena Akçer-Ön, Alan M. Greaves · The Holocene 33(7), 807–815 (2023)

Publisher / DOICode / data

What this paper does

This paper develops a regional temperature reconstruction by combining information from 33 previously published proxy-based reconstructions. The records are analysed using fully Bayesian multilevel regression within consecutive 300-year intervals, allowing site-level information to contribute to a regional estimate rather than treating each record independently.

Main finding

The framework provides a regional eastern Mediterranean temperature history with explicit uncertainty, while retaining information about variability among contributing proxy records. It is designed as a statistical synthesis of heterogeneous palaeoclimate reconstructions.

When this paper may be useful

  • Regional synthesis of heterogeneous palaeoclimate records
  • Hierarchical or multilevel Bayesian regression
  • Combining site-level reconstructions with explicit uncertainty
  • Holocene temperature reconstruction in the eastern Mediterranean
