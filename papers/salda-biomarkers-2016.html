Research article · 2016

Sedimentary lipid biomarkers in the magnesium rich and highly alkaline Lake Salda (south-western Anatolia)

Jérôme Kaiser, Zeki Bora Ön, Helge W. Arz, Sena Akçer-Ön · Journal of Limnology 75(3), 581–596 (2016)

Publisher / DOIFull text (Google Drive)

What this study does

The study characterises sedimentary lipid biomarkers in Lake Salda and evaluates which established organic geochemical palaeoclimate proxies can or cannot be transferred reliably to this extreme lacustrine setting.

Main finding

Several standard biomarker calibrations require caution in Lake Salda, while other lipid signals show promise; the work also documents unusual long-chain diols in the lake sediments.

When this paper may be useful

  • Biomarker palaeolimnology in extreme lakes
  • Testing transferability of organic geochemical proxies
  • TEX86 and brGDGT applications
  • Lake Salda environmental reconstruction
