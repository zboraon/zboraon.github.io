Research article · 2019

Temperature and precipitation variability in eastern Anatolia: Results from independent component analysis of Lake Van sediment data spanning the last 250 kyr BP

Zeki Bora Ön, M. Sinan Özeren · Quaternary International 514, 119–129 (2019)

Publisher / DOIFull text (PDF)

What this study does

The study applies independent component analysis to Lake Van proxy data to extract statistically independent environmental signals and interprets them in terms of regional temperature and precipitation variability.

Main finding

Temperature variability broadly follows large-scale glacial–interglacial patterns, while the precipitation component highlights regional hydroclimate behaviour that is not always synchronous with temperature, including changes across MIS stages and abrupt events.

When this paper may be useful

  • Blind source separation in palaeoclimate records
  • Independent component analysis of μXRF/geochemical series
  • Long Quaternary hydroclimate reconstruction
  • Separating temperature and precipitation signals
