Spectral Features of 250 kyr Long Lake Van Sediments: Milankovitch Cycles and Their Harmonics

Zeki Bora Ön, M. Sinan Özeren, Sena Akçer-Ön, M. Namık Çağatay · Geological Bulletin of Turkey 60(4), 471–488 (2017)

Publisher / DOIFull text (Google Drive)

What this study does

The study applies Lomb–Scargle spectral analysis to unevenly sampled palaeoclimate time series from Lake Van and evaluates periodicities against expected orbital and millennial-scale climate cycles.

Main finding

The spectra contain signals associated with Milankovitch-band variability and harmonics, as well as shorter Holocene periodicities; the analysis illustrates how irregularly sampled sediment records can be examined in the frequency domain.

When this paper may be useful

  • Spectral analysis of irregular palaeoclimate time series
  • Milankovitch-cycle detection
  • Long Lake Van sediment records
  • Lomb–Scargle periodograms
