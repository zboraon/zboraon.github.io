Research article · 2018

Late Pleistocene to Holocene paleoenvironmental evolution of Lake Hazar, Eastern Anatolia, Turkey

K. Kadir Eriş, Sena Akçer-Ön, M. Namık Çağatay, Umut Barış Ülgen, Zeki Bora Ön, Zülfü Gürocak, Tuğçe Nagihan Arslan, Dicle Bal Akkoca, Emre Damcı, Murat İnceöz, Özlem Öztekin Okan · Quaternary International 486, 4–16 (2018)

Publisher / DOIFull text (PDF)

What this study does

The study integrates high-resolution seismic stratigraphy with sediment-core proxies to trace major lake-level, sedimentological and climatic changes in eastern Anatolia.

Main finding

The record captures large Late Pleistocene lake-level changes and later transgression, together with signals associated with millennial-scale climate variability including Dansgaard–Oeschger and Heinrich-type events.

When this paper may be useful

  • Lake-level and palaeoenvironment reconstruction
  • Combining seismic stratigraphy and sediment cores
  • Late Pleistocene–Holocene climate in eastern Anatolia
  • Regional expression of millennial-scale climate events
