Research article · 2023

A Bayesian change point analysis re-examines the 4.2 ka BP event in southeast Europe and southwest Asia

Zeki Bora Ön · Quaternary Science Reviews 312, 108163 (2023)

Publisher / DOICodeFull text (PDF)

What this study does

Rather than assuming an abrupt change at a predetermined date, the model lets each palaeoclimate series identify whether a change point is supported and estimates its timing probabilistically.

Main finding

The analysed records do not support a spatially uniform regional event: evidence is stronger in parts of the central/eastern Mediterranean while several Anatolian and Levantine records do not display the same clear change.

When this paper may be useful

  • Testing proposed abrupt palaeoclimate events
  • Estimating change-point timing without fixing it beforehand
  • Comparing spatial coherence among records
  • Bayesian MCMC analysis of palaeoclimate time series
