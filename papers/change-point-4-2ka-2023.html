Research article · 2023

A Bayesian change point analysis re-examines the 4.2 ka BP event in southeast Europe and southwest Asia

Zeki Bora Ön · Quaternary Science Reviews 312, 108163 (2023)

Publisher / DOICode / data

What this paper does

This paper uses Bayesian change-point modelling to test palaeoclimate time series that have been cited as evidence for the 4.2 ka BP event. Rather than assuming that every record contains an abrupt change at 4.2 ka, the method lets the data identify whether and where a change point is supported.

Main finding

The results do not support a uniform regional expression of the event. Evidence is stronger in the central Mediterranean and Greece, while the analysed records from Anatolia and the Levant do not show the same clear signal, suggesting a geographically asymmetric response.

When this paper may be useful

  • Testing proposed abrupt climatic events
  • Estimating change-point timing without fixing it beforehand
  • Comparing spatial coherence of palaeoclimate signals
  • Bayesian MCMC/Gibbs approaches to environmental time series
