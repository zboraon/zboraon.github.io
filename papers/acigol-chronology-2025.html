Research article · 2025

Chronology and environmental changes from a sediment core spanning the last 485 ka from Lake Acıgöl (SW Anatolia)

Hüseyin Çaldırak, Zeki Bora Ön, Mehmet Serkan Akkiraz, M. Namık Çağatay, Bassam Ghaleb, Sabine Wulf, Kadir Eriş, Dursun Acar, Jérôme Kaiser, Sena Akçer-Ön · Quaternary International 722, 109710 (2025)

Publisher / DOIRepository copy

What this study does

The study combines radiometric constraints, tephrochronology, stratigraphic information and alignment/tuning approaches within an age-depth framework for the Acıgöl core, and evaluates environmental changes using sedimentary and palynological evidence.

Main finding

The chronology places the analysed sequence across multiple glacial–interglacial cycles (MIS 1–12 and part of MIS 13), providing the temporal framework needed for quantitative and multiproxy palaeoclimate analysis of the basin.

When this paper may be useful

  • Long Quaternary lake-core chronologies
  • Age-depth modelling with multiple chronological constraints
  • Glacial–interglacial environmental change in Anatolia
  • Lake Acıgöl palaeoclimate studies
