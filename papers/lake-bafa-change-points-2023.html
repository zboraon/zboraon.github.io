Research article · 2023

Dates of Holocene environmental changes in Lake Bafa: A hierarchical Bayesian analysis of change points

Zeki Bora Ön · Turkish Geographical Review 82, 23–36 (2023)

Publisher / DOI

What this paper does

This study introduces a hierarchical Bayesian change-point analysis for identifying environmental tipping points from several palaeoenvironmental time series. It is applied to stable oxygen and carbon isotopes from foraminifera and ostracods together with total organic carbon from the BAFA09P02 sediment core.

Main finding

The analysis provides probabilistic estimates for the timing of major environmental transitions associated with the separation of Lake Bafa from the Aegean Sea and its subsequent development. The focus is on objectively estimating transition timing and uncertainty from multiple proxy records.

When this paper may be useful

  • Multi-proxy environmental transition dating
  • Hierarchical Bayesian change-point analysis
  • Lake-basin evolution and marine-to-lacustrine transitions
  • Quantifying uncertainty around tipping-point dates
