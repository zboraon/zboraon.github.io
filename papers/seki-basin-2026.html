Research article · 2026

Holocene vegetation change, climate variability, and human impacts on the Seki Basin (southwestern Türkiye): Evidence from a multiproxy approach

Zahra Hashemi, Demet Biltekin, Alan M. Greaves, Sena Akçer-Ön, Zeki Bora Ön, Memet Namık Çağatay, Ömer Yetemen · Palaeogeography, Palaeoclimatology, Palaeoecology 700, 114053 (2026)

Publisher / DOIRepository copy

What this study does

The study combines pollen, sediment geochemistry and organic/inorganic carbon information from a 4.7 m sediment sequence to reconstruct environmental change from the terminal Pleistocene through the Holocene.

Main finding

The record documents a cold/arid Younger Dryas followed by warmer and wetter early-Holocene conditions, while anthropogenic indicators become increasingly important during the later Holocene.

When this paper may be useful

  • Holocene vegetation and climate in southwestern Anatolia
  • Pollen–geochemistry multiproxy comparisons
  • Separating climatic and human influences
  • Regional archaeological–environmental synthesis
