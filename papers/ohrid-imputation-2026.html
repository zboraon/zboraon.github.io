Bayesian stochastic imputation of discontinuous paleoenvironmental proxy records: a case study from Lake Ohrid stable isotopes

Zeki Bora Ön · Environmental and Ecological Statistics 33, 129–148 (2026)

Publisher / DOICodeFull text (PDF)

What this study does

This study develops a modular Bayesian workflow for discontinuous proxy records. A latent Gaussian state-space model regularises irregularly sampled series and represents uncertainty; Bayesian multivariate regression with stochastic search variable selection then uses informative coeval proxies to reconstruct missing observations.

Main finding

The framework reconstructs gaps in Lake Ohrid δ18O and δ13C over a long Quaternary sequence and returns posterior uncertainty for the imputed values rather than a single deterministic interpolation.

When this paper may be useful

  • Missing values in palaeoclimate or environmental proxy records
  • Reconstruction of unevenly or irregularly sampled observations onto a regular temporal grid
  • Bayesian state-space modelling of multivariate environmental time series
  • Stochastic search variable selection (SSVS) for identifying informative predictors
  • Propagation of imputation and reconstruction uncertainty
