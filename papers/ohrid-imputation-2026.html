Research article · 2026

Bayesian stochastic imputation of discontinuous paleoenvironmental proxy records: a case study from Lake Ohrid stable isotopes

Zeki Bora Ön · Environmental and Ecological Statistics 33, 129–148 (2026)

Publisher / DOI

What this paper does

This paper develops a modular two-stage Bayesian framework for reconstructing gaps in discontinuous palaeoenvironmental proxy records. A latent Gaussian state-space model first places irregularly sampled proxy series onto a common grid and represents uncertainty. A multivariate Bayesian regression with stochastic search variable selection (SSVS) then identifies informative coeval proxies and reconstructs missing values.

Main finding

Applied to Lake Ohrid, the framework reconstructs gaps in δ18O and δ13C over the last 624 ka and yields credible intervals for the imputed values. The reconstructed series retain coherent glacial–interglacial variability and provide a probabilistic way to bridge intervals where endogenic calcite is absent.

When this paper may be useful

  • Missing values in palaeoclimate or environmental proxy records
  • Irregularly sampled multivariate time series
  • State-space modelling and latent-process reconstruction
  • Stochastic search variable selection (SSVS)
  • Propagation of observation and reconstruction uncertainty
