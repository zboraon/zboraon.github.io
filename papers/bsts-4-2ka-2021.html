Research article · 2021

A Bayesian test for the 4.2 ka BP abrupt climatic change event in southeast Europe and southwest Asia using structural time series analysis of paleoclimate data

Zeki Bora Ön, Alan M. Greaves, Sena Akçer-Ön, M. Sinan Özeren · Climatic Change 165(1–2), Article 7 (2021)

Publisher / DOICode / data

What this paper does

This study applies Bayesian structural time-series modelling and the CausalImpact framework to palaeoclimate records from southeast Europe and southwest Asia. For each target series, a synthetic counterfactual is constructed from comparison series and used to test whether an anomaly around 4.2 ka BP is credibly different from the expected trajectory.

Main finding

Some records support a climatic anomaly around 4.2 ka BP, while others do not show a credible effect. The results therefore argue against assuming that the event had a spatially uniform expression across the study region.

When this paper may be useful

  • Bayesian structural time-series models
  • Counterfactual/causal-impact analysis of palaeoclimate records
  • Testing hypothesised abrupt events
  • Regional comparison of environmental time series
