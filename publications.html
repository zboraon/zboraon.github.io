Publications

Selected papers with short explanations of the scientific question, statistical approach and situations in which each study may be useful.

Bayesian stochastic imputation of discontinuous paleoenvironmental proxy records: a case study from Lake Ohrid stable isotopes

Zeki Bora Ön · Environmental and Ecological Statistics · 2026 · DOI 10.1007/s10651-025-00701-6

A modular Bayesian two-stage framework for imputing missing palaeoenvironmental proxy values while propagating uncertainty, demonstrated on Lake Ohrid stable isotopes.

A conceptual and statistical framework for delineating the timing of a stratigraphic transition: Holocene-Anthropocene boundary as a case study

Zeki Bora Ön, Mustafa Efe Ateş, Jérôme Kaiser · Progress in Physical Geography: Earth and Environment · 2025 · DOI 10.1177/03091333241310742

A multilevel Bayesian change-point and errors-in-variables framework for estimating a stratigraphic transition from multiple records, demonstrated with Anthropocene markers.

A novel Bayesian multilevel regression approach to the reconstruction of an eastern Mediterranean temperature record for the last 10,000 years

Zeki Bora Ön, Neil Macdonald, Sena Akçer-Ön, Alan M. Greaves · The Holocene · 2023 · DOI 10.1177/09596836231163508

A fully Bayesian multilevel regression synthesis of 33 proxy-based temperature reconstructions for the eastern Mediterranean over the last 10,000 years.

A Bayesian change point analysis re-examines the 4.2 ka BP event in southeast Europe and southwest Asia

Zeki Bora Ön · Quaternary Science Reviews · 2023 · DOI 10.1016/j.quascirev.2023.108163

A fully Bayesian change-point test of palaeoclimate records used to support the 4.2 ka BP event across southeast Europe and southwest Asia.

Dates of Holocene environmental changes in Lake Bafa: A hierarchical Bayesian analysis of change points

Zeki Bora Ön · Turkish Geographical Review · 2023 · DOI 10.17211/tcd.1283443

A hierarchical Bayesian change-point approach for estimating Holocene environmental transitions in Lake Bafa from multiple proxy records.

A Bayesian test for the 4.2 ka BP abrupt climatic change event in southeast Europe and southwest Asia using structural time series analysis of paleoclimate data

Zeki Bora Ön, Alan M. Greaves, Sena Akçer-Ön, M. Sinan Özeren · Climatic Change · 2021 · DOI 10.1007/s10584-021-03010-6

A Bayesian structural time-series and causal-impact test of palaeoclimate records proposed as evidence for the 4.2 ka BP climate event.
